Neon Harbor: One Night to Buy Back the Gullwife. An original open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser. Roam the neon port city of Kestrel Bay on foot and by car as Ivo Lark, a washed-up harbour pilot working one night for fixer Odette Fenn to buy back his sister's impounded boat before its dawn auction. Steal any car, run four story missions and repeatable cab fares, and stay ahead of a wanted system that escalates from one star to five-star Interceptor chases, with traffic, pedestrians, combat, a live minimap, saves, and synthesized sound. Keyboard and mouse, no network, no installs.

## How to play

- **WASD or arrow keys** — move on foot, or steer when driving
- **Shift** — sprint
- **E** — enter a car, or steal one (parked or with its driver still inside)
- **F** — melee attack
- **Mouse** — aim; **click** to shoot
- **Esc or P** — pause

Take jobs from Odette Fenn, earn cash, and keep the heat off long enough to reach the Pier 9 finale before dawn.

## Features

- Procedural neon port city: 9 avenues by 7 streets with lit blocks and neon signs, Gull Square, the Lantern Market, Fathom Park, car parks, a marina, a beach and boardwalk, and three piers on animated water
- Living traffic that follows the lane network and brakes for trouble, plus pedestrians who wander, flee gunfire, and dive out of the way of cars
- Car stealing with two-circle driving physics, drift, and skid marks; any parked or traffic car can be taken
- Five-star wanted system: witnessed crimes raise your heat, police Interceptors hunt you down, and a bust meter fills when you are slow or cornered — heat cools off when nobody is watching
- Four story missions, from a crate delivery to a timed Pier 9 finale under a three-star floor, plus the repeatable Gull Cab fare job
- Mouse-aimed hitscan gun combat with tracers and muzzle flash, and F-key melee; hostile Combine heavies fight back
- HUD with health, cash, car condition and speed, wanted stars, bust bar, objective banner, and a live minimap with player, objective, cab, and police blips
- Title, pause, BUSTED, and WASTED screens, plus a dawn ending card with your run stats
- localStorage saves with Continue or New Night
- Fully synthesized WebAudio sound — engine, sirens, gunshots, impacts, and a bass-and-pad music loop; no audio files
- Runs entirely offline from static files: no network requests, no installs

## Notes

Neon Harbor is an original work — its city, characters, story, and art were created for this game — and it has no affiliation with Rockstar Games or the Grand Theft Auto series.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
